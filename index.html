Coffee Production Process

The main stages of coffee production are:

  1. Planting coffee seedlings
  2. Caring for the coffee plants
  3. Harvesting ripe coffee cherries
  4. Processing and drying the coffee beans
  5. Packaging the coffee beans

Plantation Activities

Pruning
removing unwanted branches to help coffee plants grow well and remain healthy.
Harvesting
picking ripe coffee cherries from the coffee plants.
Drying
reducing moisture in cofee beans to prepare them for storage and further processing.
